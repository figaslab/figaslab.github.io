Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers figaslab ("we", "us"): this website and the figaslab video uploader, the internal tool we use to publish our game trailers on YouTube. Contact: figas.dev@gmail.com.

Our games

What our Android games collect, and why, is described in full in the figaslab games privacy policy.

YouTube API Services

The figaslab video uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload gameplay trailers of our own games to our own YouTube channel. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

  • Who uses it: only figaslab, signed in to our own channel account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • What it accesses: one OAuth permission, youtube.upload, used solely to upload videos we made. It does not read, collect or analyse any YouTube data about other users, channels or videos.
  • What it stores: the OAuth refresh token, and the ID and URL YouTube returns for each video we upload. Both stay in local files on our own computer; they are never sent to any server other than Google's and never shared with third parties.
  • Cookies: neither this website nor the tool sets cookies.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page, https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • When access is revoked, or on request to figas.dev@gmail.com, we delete the stored token and the stored video IDs within 7 days. Videos already published stay on YouTube until removed there.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page, with a new date at the top.
