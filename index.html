Free board and puzzle games for Android. Play a friend next to you over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, no internet needed, or practise against the CPU.

BattleshipHide your fleet, hunt theirs. CheckersThe classic, with solo puzzles to sharpen your jumps. ChessPlay a friend or the CPU, and solve chess puzzles. Dots and BoxesDraw lines, close boxes, take the extra turn. Four in a RowDrop discs and line up four before they do. GomokuFive stones in a row wins. HexLink your two sides of the board before your rival links theirs. Lane MathMerge numbered blocks and bank the biggest piles. Lane RushClear the board one arrow at a time, faster than your rival. Letter RushSwipe hidden words out of the letter grid. MahjongMatch free tiles and clear the layout. MancalaSow seeds, chain extra turns, capture. Match TenFind pairs that add up to 10. Nine Men's MorrisForm mills, take men, block your rival. Quarry DoorRace to the door, build walls, play action cards. ReversiOutflank and flip your way to the most discs. Sinuca BrasilBrazilian snooker, solo or head to head.
